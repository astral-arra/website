### What are telesmatic objects?

Telesmatic objects are magical items created through a ritual or process whose purpose is to charge them with specific energies or spiritual forces. The word comes from the Greek *telesma*, meaning "consecration" or "completion". These objects serve as vessels or ***conduits for particular intentions, spirits or forces*** and are used in various forms of magic and esoteric traditions such as [Hermeticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermeticism), [Kabbalah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kabbalah) and [alchemy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alchemy).

### Features of telesmatic objects

- **Created with purpose**

   Telesmatic objects are made deliberately to channel or direct energy towards a specific goal – protection, healing, prosperity or spiritual growth.

- **Steeped in symbolism**

   They usually carry symbols, sigils or inscriptions tied to the intention – astrological signs, planetary influences, sacred geometry and so on.

- **Choice of material**

   The material is chosen for its energetic qualities – gold for solar energy and power, silver for intuition, crystals for healing and transformation, and so on.

- **Ritual charging and enchantment**

   Through ritual, meditation or focused intention, the object is charged with the desired power. This can involve visualisations, incantations or the use of natural and astrological energies, as well as spoken enchantment.

- **Practical use**

   Telesmatic items are worn as amulets, placed on altars or used actively during rituals. They radiate the accumulated energy into the space or act directly on their owner.

### Examples of telesmatic objects

- **Amulets and talismans** – for protection, love, abundance, success.
- **[Sigils and symbols](/en/magic)** – activated through ritual for a specific wish.
- **Magical tools** – wands, knives (athames), chalices, if ritually charged.
- **Crystals and stones** – programmed with energy for a targeted effect.

### How to create a telesmatic object – step by step

1. **Choose the item and material**

   Match them to the goal – for example, a red thread for protection or rose quartz for love.

2. **Inscribe the symbolism**

   Sigils, names of powers, planetary symbols or personal codes.

3. **Ritual consecration**

   The presence of the four elements, and possibly the invocation of astral or divine forces.

4. **Charge it with intention**

   Through visualisation, breath, ritual, light (sun/moon) or an astrological event.

5. **Use and care**

   The item is worn, kept in a special place or used in rituals. It needs periodic recharging or cleansing.

## Knot Magic: Tying and Untying Fate

### What is knot magic?

A special branch of telesmatic magic is so-called knot magic.

Knot magic is a form of folk magic that uses **knots** to **bind, hold or release energy** around a particular theme – love, protection, success, blessing and more. It is an extremely ancient technique, found in Celtic and Norse traditions, witchcraft and Sufi magic. Today witches of every paradigm use it in their practice.

### How do you do knot magic?

1. **Choose a thread or cord**

   Remember that the colour symbolises the intention and the energy of the wish:

   - Red – passion, strength
   - Green – money, healing
   - Black – protection, ending
   - White – purity, new beginnings, and so on.

2. **Set your intention**

   In your mind or out loud, state what will be "bound" into each knot. You do this while making the knot. Speak the "binding" as you tie each one.

3. **Incantation – a ritual with every tie**

   With every knot you say a phrase or visualise the goal.

Here's an example:

> First knot – my will takes hold in the world.  
> Second knot – fate falls into line with my will…

and so on. You get the idea.

4. **Charge it with energy**

   The final tie is sealed with the energy of the person doing the enchantment. That is exactly what makes the talisman strong and working in favour of whoever enchanted it. The talisman can be worn, placed in a particular spot or sealed in a box – and there are quite a few subtleties here that I won't reveal.

5. **Untying (optional)**

   When the goal is reached, or when circumstances call for the energy to be released, the knots are untied with the intention of release. This also follows a particular ritual.
