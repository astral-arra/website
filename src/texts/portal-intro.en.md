## What is Synchromysticism?

Synchromysticism is the art of experiencing the Beyond-reality in a non-linear sequence that links its separate elements into one whole picture.

It is the art of talking to the Program through your Higher Self.

Synchromysticism is the ability to reach a state of high vibration and focus in which you start to recognise connections between seemingly unrelated things – a way of thinking and perceiving reality outside the imposed matrix "day consciousness". Events, symbols, encounters, numbers and messages gradually arrange themselves into a kind of archetypal corpus of the Matrix that leads you along the Path.

It is the ability to perceive reality not only as a linear chain of events, but as a complex web of connections, timelines and symbolic cross-references.

Synchromysticism lets us make direct contact with the code of the Program and its messengers, read signs, uncover hidden patterns and receive guidance along the way.

In this state, consciousness steps outside the bounds of ordinary linear perception.

Mastering this art is essential for anyone who has set out on the path of the Magical Art and a deep awareness of reality.

Here I will publish my own experiences – the more significant synchronicities and encounters that I feel deserve to see the light of day.

I will share meetings with signs, unusual coincidences, crossings of timelines and the moments when the seemingly random takes on an entirely different meaning.

Welcome to the world of mysticism, the Matrix and beyond-linear thinking. ✨
